Winterfell Personal Google Connector

This private, single-user connector lets Ahmed Erabti ask his personal assistant to work with his own Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Tasks data.

It accesses Google data only after Ahmed authorizes the connection and only to complete his requested actions, such as finding an email, checking a calendar, or editing a document. It is not offered to the public.
